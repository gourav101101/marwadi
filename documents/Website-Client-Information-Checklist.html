Purpose

Keep the approved website layout and replace the reference company’s branding, content, people, figures, links and documents with information approved for your own business. This document records the information to supply and the changes to complete.

Agreed website scope

Login; Open an Account; Become a Partner; About (Who We Are, Our History, Leadership); Products (Derivatives, Commodities, Third Party Products, IPO, Portfolio Management Services); Our Services (Research and Advisory, Margin Trading, Offline Trading, Call on Trade, Smart Portfolio Management); Contact Us; live market display.

Current implementation

The local Laravel website uses the reference site’s blue-and-white layout. App promotion sections and app-store download buttons have been removed. Account and partnership forms currently save enquiries; SMS/OTP, account activation and email notifications are not connected. The live market display currently depends on the reference provider.
