When you are unable to access the Internet or your computer, you can trade on the stock market using your mobile phone. You can buy and sell a variety of securities, including equity, mutual funds, derivatives, initial public offerings (IPOs), and many others.

  • There is no limit to the number of orders that can be placed for purchasing and selling.
  • You can place numerous orders at the same time.
  • A multi-layered verification mechanism eliminates the possibility of fraud.
  • This facility does not require any paperwork.
  • You will receive order status information such as confirmations and order flow details.
